Taliesin West

Taliesin West is Frank Lloyd Wright’s winter home and studio in northeast Scottsdale, Arizona. Built beginning in 1937, it served as a desert laboratory for his architectural ideas and later became a school for architecture.

The complex sits in the foothills of the McDowell Mountains and is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site and National Historic Landmark.

How to Get There

Taliesin West is located near the intersection of N Frank Lloyd Wright Boulevard and Cactus Road in Scottsdale.

  • Address: 12621 N Frank Lloyd Wright Blvd, Scottsdale, AZ 85259
  • From central Phoenix: Drive east on Loop 202 or Loop 101, then take local streets north toward Frank Lloyd Wright Blvd.
  • From Scottsdale: Use local streets to head northeast toward the McDowell Mountains and follow signs for Taliesin West.

The easiest way to navigate is to enter “Taliesin West Scottsdale AZ” into a maps or navigation app and follow the driving directions.

Design and the Desert

Wright designed Taliesin West to grow out of the desert landscape. Its low, angular walls are built from local stone and concrete, and the buildings are oriented to capture long views while protecting occupants from the intense sun.

Deep roof overhangs, shaded walkways, and outdoor rooms demonstrate how architecture can respond to climate, light, and topography instead of fighting against them.

Visiting Today

Today Taliesin West is home to the Frank Lloyd Wright Foundation and offers guided tours that explore the drafting studio, living spaces, and surrounding desert.

For current tour options, ticket prices, and hours, visit the official Taliesin West website.
